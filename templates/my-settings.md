## My Dopamenu

A dopamenu is a pre-made list of activities sorted by effort level. When your brain is stuck, you don't have to *think of* something to do — you just pick from the menu.

### Appetizers (5-15 min, low effort)

Quick hits for when you need a small win or a reset.

- Make a cup of coffee or tea
- Step outside and walk to the end of the block
-
-

### Entrees (15-45 min, moderate effort)

Activities that can get you into a light flow state.

- Work on a side project with no deadline
- Cook a meal from a recipe you already know
-
-

### Sides (pair with other tasks)

Things you can do *while* doing something else.

- Listen to a podcast or playlist
- Fidget toy or doodle pad
-
-

### Desserts (special treats, use sparingly)

High-dopamine rewards you save for after hard things.

- Play a favorite video game for one session
- Order from that restaurant you like
-
-

### Specials (longer, high reward)

Bigger investments that reliably recharge you.

- Day trip somewhere new
- Deep-dive into a hobby project all afternoon
-
-
