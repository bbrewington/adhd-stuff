# Emotion Tracker Template

Source: How to ADHD - "ADHD and Emotional Dysregulation: What You Need to Know"

## Fields

| Field | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| When did it happen? | Date/time or general timeframe | Tuesday afternoon |
| What was the situation/trigger? | What was happening externally | Got critical feedback on a PR at work |
| What did you feel? | Name the emotion(s) - use the feelings wheel if needed | Shame, frustration, inadequacy |
| How did you react? | What you did in response | Shut down, stopped responding to Slack for 2 hours |
| What were the consequences? | Good or bad outcomes of the reaction | Missed a standup; but also took a walk that helped |

## Usage Notes

- Fill this out after the fact, not during the emotional moment
- The goal is pattern recognition over time, not self-judgment
- Look for recurring triggers and reactions after 1-2 weeks of tracking
- Pair with the Coping Skills Log when you start practicing new responses
